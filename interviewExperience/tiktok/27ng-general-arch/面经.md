## 2. Interview process (from 2026 NG reports)

Typical pipeline (varies by team; OA sometimes waived for strong resumes / recruiter-sourced candidates):

| Stage | Format | Length | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| OA | CodeSignal | 70 min | 4 algorithm questions (may be waived) |
| Round 1 | Lark video | 60 min | Coding (1–2 LC medium) + light BQ + CS fundamentals |
| Round 2 | Lark video | 60 min | ~30 min resume deep dive + ~30 min coding (often practical: LRU, rate limiter, cache) |
| Round 3 | Lark video | 60 min | System design (NG-lite) and/or project deep dive with senior/HM |
| Round 4 / HR | Lark video | 30–45 min | HM + BQ: why TikTok, proudest project, failure, conflict, career plans |

Key observations from multiple candidate reports:

- **Interviews are on Lark (飞书)** — install it and test camera/mic beforehand.
- Rounds are scheduled one at a time; pass one to get the next. Whole cycle 3–8 weeks.
- Coding: LeetCode Hot 100 territory, mostly **Medium**; efficiency and clean narration matter. Backend-flavored practical problems are common: **LRU cache (+ "make it thread-safe" follow-up), rate limiter, cache system**.
- Follow-ups are relentless: after solving, expect "prove correctness", "what if multithreaded", "edge cases before you say done".
- CS fundamentals in the ByteDance style: HashMap/ConcurrentHashMap internals, thread pools, TCP handshake/teardown, HTTPS, MySQL indexes/MVCC/isolation levels, Redis persistence + cache penetration/breakdown/avalanche, cache-DB consistency.
- System design for NG is "lite": high-level architecture (gateway/service/DB/cache), bottlenecks (hot keys, read-heavy), rough numbers (QPS, storage). Past questions: feed, like/follow service, short-video upload+transcode, notification service, URL shortener, **distributed LLM conversation-context service** (very relevant to this team).
- BQ is concentrated in the HM/HR round but sprinkled everywhere. Standard set: tell me about yourself (30-sec pitch), why TikTok, proudest project with quantified impact, a failure + reflection, a conflict (STAR).
- Interviewers give hints and confirm answers in real time; they care that you **communicate trade-offs out loud** more than that you finish fast.
- For this AI-infra team specifically, expect scenario questions like: "How would you use LLM agents to reduce CI time / auto-fix a failing build / refactor a large legacy codebase safely?" — your Microsoft internship is the perfect anchor.
